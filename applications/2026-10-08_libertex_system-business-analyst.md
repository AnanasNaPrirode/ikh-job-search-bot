We are looking for a System Business Analyst in the data department to bridge communication between our business and development teams, translating business needs into clear technical specifications.

- Collaborate with stakeholders to identify, analyze, and document business requirements.

- Translate business needs into acceptance criteria and technical specifications for the development team.
- Analyze, define, and document detailed system requirements, including functional, non-functional, and integration requirements.
- Lead system configuration and data mapping efforts, particularly those involving APIs, third-party platforms, and internal system integrations.
- Support post-release validation and acceptance of features to ensure solutions meet stakeholder expectations.
